1-Command Router Installation

Pick your operating system below to SSH into your AW1000 router and install in under 15 seconds.

Windows 10 / 11 (PowerShell or Windows Terminal)

1. Connect your PC to the AW1000 Wi-Fi or LAN cable, open PowerShell, and log in:

ssh root@192.168.1.1

2. Once logged into the router, paste this 1-command installer:

wget -qO /tmp/aw1000-control-router-v1.0.5.tar.gz https://github.com/sky12388/aw1000-control/releases/download/v1.0.5/aw1000-control-router-v1.0.5.tar.gz && wget -qO /tmp/install.sh https://github.com/sky12388/aw1000-control/releases/download/v1.0.5/install.sh && sh /tmp/install.sh /tmp/aw1000-control-router-v1.0.5.tar.gz

Or install via OpenWrt opkg:

wget -qO /tmp/aw1000-control_1.0.5_all.ipk https://github.com/sky12388/aw1000-control/releases/download/v1.0.5/aw1000-control_1.0.5_all.ipk && opkg install /tmp/aw1000-control_1.0.5_all.ipk
Cross-Platform Browser Access

Windows, macOS, Linux, and iOS users don't even need an app — just open https://192.168.1.1/app/ (or your VPN IP) directly in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
