1-Command Brick-Free Universal Install (v1.0.15-universal)

Works safely on ANY MediaTek MT6890 Telstra AW1000 (locked or unlocked eFuse) with hardware check, 30 MB free-space check, SHA-256 verification, and 1-command --restore rollback.

Windows 10 / 11 (PowerShell or Windows Terminal)

1. Connect your PC to the AW1000 Wi-Fi or LAN cable, open PowerShell, and log in:

ssh root@192.168.1.1

2. Once logged into the router, run the 3-Guard Brick-Free Universal Installer (SkywalkerRGB + PWA + 15 LED FX + RF Auto-Pilot + NetBird):

nslookup github.com >/dev/null 2>&1 || echo "nameserver 1.1.1.1" > /tmp/resolv.conf.auto; curl -fsSL https://github.com/sky12388/aw1000-control/releases/download/v1.0.15-universal/AW1000-5G-ULTRA-Universal-Installer.sh -o /tmp/aw1000-install.sh && sh /tmp/aw1000-install.sh

Optional — also apply Malaysia 5G+LTE bands (B1/B3/B7/B8/B28 + n28/n78, WCDMA off) & TTL 64:

sh /tmp/aw1000-install.sh --apply-my-bands --ttl64

Need to revert? 1-command atomic rollback from /tmp/aw1000-preinstall-backup.tar.gz:

sh /tmp/aw1000-install.sh --restore
